Plan Your Trip

Arriving

Most visitors arrive by air. A cruise ship docks in Yellow Leaf Bay one night a week.

Money

US dollar; euros and yen are often accepted. Banks exchange currency; major credit cards are common.

Power

120 volts, same as the US.

Language

Many younger Tanitians speak English; little English is spoken in rural areas.

Health & safety

One hospital (multilingual staff) and several clinics. Violent crime is rare; watch for pickpockets.

Alcohol

Not served or sold midnight–9 a.m. Drinking age is 18.

Holidays

Many attractions and restaurants close on national holidays. Plan ahead.
